Why India, why now

The population that will use a test should be the one it is validated in

Most of the field's reference cohorts were built outside India. Diet, environment and medication shape the gut microbiome far more than host genetics does, so a signature built elsewhere has to be re-established here before it can guide care.

The groups that run those first Indian validation studies set the reference standard for everyone who follows. Mahanim Biologics is built to be the analytical partner in those studies, and to hold a stake in what they produce.
